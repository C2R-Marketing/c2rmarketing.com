What this checks

AI search engines read your site differently from people. This tool checks whether your site is reachable, whether it has an llms.txt file (the signpost AI crawlers look for), whether your pages describe themselves clearly (title, description, headings), whether structured data helps AI understand your business, and whether your robots.txt accidentally blocks AI crawlers.
