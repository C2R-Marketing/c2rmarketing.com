What does Yerushalayim mean?

יְרוּשָׁלַיִם
Yerushalayim
say: yeh-roo-shah-LYE-eem

What English prints

Your English Bible prints "Jerusalem" where the Hebrew has Yerushalayim.

What is hidden

foundation of peace.

The teaching note

The city English calls Jerusalem is, in Hebrew, a sentence: the foundation (or teaching) of peace. The name is even written in a plural form — one city, layered meanings. Say it slowly in the trainer; the length is the lesson.
